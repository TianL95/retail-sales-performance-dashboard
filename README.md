# Retail Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyse retail sales and profitability. This project demonstrates data preparation, KPI reporting, data visualisation, and interactive business reporting.

## Dashboard Preview

![Retail Sales Performance Dashboard](images/retail_sales_dashboard.png)

## Business Questions

- How do sales change over time?
- Which product categories generate the highest sales?
- How does sales performance vary across regions?
- Which 10 products have the highest sales?
- How can users explore performance by year and category?

## Key Performance Indicators

- **Total Sales** — overall sales revenue.
- **Total Profit** — overall profit.
- **Number of Orders** — distinct order count.

## Dashboard Features

- Monthly sales trend analysis.
- Sales comparison by product category.
- Regional sales performance comparison.
- Top 10 products ranked by sales.
- Interactive Year and Category slicers.
- Consistent KPI formatting and business-focused visualisation.

## Tools and Skills

- **Power BI Desktop:** dashboard development and visualisation.
- **Power Query:** data preparation and data type validation.
- **Data Analysis:** sales, profit, order and product performance.
- **Business Intelligence:** interactive reporting and KPI analysis.

## Key Insights and Recommendations

## Key Insights

Based on the dashboard analysis, the following insights were identified:

- **Overall performance:** The business generated approximately **$2.30 million in sales** and **$286,400 in profit**, across approximately **5,009 distinct orders**.
- **Category performance:** Technology recorded the highest sales, followed by Furniture and Office Supplies.
- **Regional performance:** The West region had the highest sales, followed by East, Central and South. Sales performance varied across regions, indicating opportunities for more targeted regional strategies.
- **Monthly sales trends:** Sales fluctuated over time, with noticeable peaks and declines. The chart shows a strong increase toward the end of the displayed period.
- **Product performance:** The Top 10 Products chart highlights the products contributing the most to sales, helping identify key products for inventory and sales planning.

## Business Recommendations

1. **Strengthen Technology sales:** Investigate the products and customer demand driving Technology's leading sales performance, and evaluate opportunities to sustain growth.
2. **Review regional performance:** Investigate the factors contributing to the West region's strong sales and assess opportunities to improve performance in the South region.
3. **Investigate sales seasonality:** Review monthly sales patterns to support demand forecasting, inventory planning and promotional scheduling.
4. **Optimise product planning:** Use the Top 10 Products analysis to support inventory decisions and identify opportunities for complementary product sales.
5. **Monitor profitability:** Analyse profit margins, discounts and costs by category, region and product before making investment decisions. High sales do not necessarily mean high profitability.

## Conclusion
